# Removal guide

1. Export or copy the Markdown files and allowed attachments you need.
2. Disable Templater and Dataview, then confirm the notes remain readable.
3. Remove the included `.obsidian` settings if you do not want them applied.
4. Delete the fictional examples and templates you do not use.
5. Remove the vault directory only after opening the retained copy and checking
   a sample of links, dates, and frontmatter.

No Software Sausage account or hosted service is required to keep the files.
