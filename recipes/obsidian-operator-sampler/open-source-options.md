# Open-source options

- [SilverBullet](https://silverbullet.md/) keeps Markdown-oriented notes and
  adds programmable queries; test Obsidian-flavored links and plugins.
- [Logseq](https://github.com/logseq/logseq) is outliner-first and supports
  local files; its block model and properties are not a drop-in vault restore.
- [Zettlr](https://www.zettlr.com/) is a Markdown editor focused on writing and
  citation workflows; it does not reproduce Dataview or Templater automation.

These are portability paths, not equivalent replacements. Test a representative
copy and keep the original readable until the destination passes its checks.
