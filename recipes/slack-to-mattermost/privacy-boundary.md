# Privacy and safety boundary

A Slack export can contain employee messages, direct messages, files, personal
data, client information, credentials, and legally sensitive records. Do not
upload a real export to a public repository, general-purpose AI service, or
unapproved migration provider.

- Confirm legal authority, notices, retention, and access before exporting.
- Work in an encrypted, access-controlled staging environment.
- Keep the original archive unchanged and record its hash.
- Never use production credentials in a public fixture or support request.
- Treat migration tools and transformed output as sensitive data.
- Delete temporary copies according to the approved retention plan.
- Test restoration and rollback before deleting the source workspace.

This kit calculates planning cost and scopes work. It does not perform a
migration, determine legal authority, or certify security or compliance.
